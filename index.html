Good to know

Your questions, answered.

A few things worth knowing before you make JOVÉELINK your home.

Who is JOVÉELINK for?

JOVÉELINK is a home for everyone in the nail industry: clients who love discovering nail art, nail artists who want to share their craft, and salon owners who want to connect with a wider community. Everyone has a place here.

Is JOVÉELINK only for booking?

Not at all. Booking is one part of JOVÉELINK. The community, with its discussions, inspiration and connections between artists and clients, is the heart of what we’re building. Nail professionals and clients come here to belong, not just to transact.

Can I preview nails before I book?

Yes. The AI and AR nail preview feature lets you explore a look before discussing it with your artist. Think of it as inspiration made personal, something to bring to your consultation.

How does JOVÉELINK support nail professionals?

Professionals can build and showcase their portfolio, present their services, manage their schedule and stay connected with clients and peers, all in one place designed for the nail industry.
