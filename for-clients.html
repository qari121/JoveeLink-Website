Everything you can do

The nail appointment, without the phone tag.

See real work. Pick a real slot. Message your artist. Pay for what you actually got. That’s it.

A map of salons around you

Real nail salons on a live map with the nearest one always on screen. Search by name or location. Tap a pin to open the salon or an artist.

Portfolios that mean something

Every artist has photos of their actual work, their prices, their specialties, their reviews. Know exactly what you’re booking before you commit.

Pick a real slot

Choose from the artist’s actual open times. Slots already taken are marked. No “we’ll call you back,” no phone tag.

Chat before you show up

Send a reference photo, agree on the design, sort out the details — one thread with your artist, nothing lost in DMs.

Pay after, not before

Invite a friend, both win

Share your referral code. They get money off their first appointment. You get a reward once they’ve actually been in — no gaming it, no cap on how many friends.

Two ways to book

Pick your artist, or let the salon pick for you.

Some days you know exactly who you want. Other days you just want the appointment. Jovée Link handles both.

Option 1

Pick your artist

Browse portfolios, prices and reviews. Choose your artist, pick a slot from their real availability, add any notes. They confirm, and you’re booked.

Option 2

Let the salon pick

Tell the salon the service and the time you want. They match you with an artist who’s free. If that artist can’t take it, it goes back to the salon instead of dying on you.
