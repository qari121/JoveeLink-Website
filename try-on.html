Virtual try-on

See it on your own hands before you book.

Try polish colours and 3D nail shapes on your own hands, right here in your browser. You don’t need to install anything or make an account.

Works in your browser Your video stays on your phone Polish and 3D shapes

Your browser will ask to use your camera. The video stays on your device. Built by Jovée AR, the company behind Jovée Link.

How it works

Getting started

1

Let it use your camera

Point the camera at your hand. The video is processed on your phone and never sent anywhere.

2

Pick a colour

Switch between polish and 3D nail shapes. You can also grab a colour off anything in your camera.

3

Book it

Found a set you like? Open the app and book a nail artist near you who does that kind of work.

Open it on your phone

Scan the code to open this page on your phone, where the camera is. The app is where you find a nail artist near you, message them, book a time and pay after your appointment.
