Everything you get

A shopfront that does the boring parts for you.

Your profile is your business. Portfolio, prices, availability and reviews all on one page — plus the reminders, chase-ups and receipts that used to eat your evening.

A portfolio that books work

Photos of your sets, your services with prices, your specialties and your reviews — all on one page clients can open from a link or QR code.

You decide when you work

Set each day as available or off, with your own hours and how many clients you take. Clients only see slots inside your real availability.

Bill for what you actually did

Finish the appointment, add the lines, send it. The client pays in the app or you mark it paid in person. Add-ons get billed with the rest — no awkward adjustments.

Turn a chat into a booking

Send a client an offer in the conversation with your price and a time. They accept in-chat and the booking is made.

Work inside a real salon

Search salons near you and ask to join their team. The salon takes bookings and hands the right ones to you — a professional home for your work, not a marketplace of strangers.

The app chases for you

Appointment reminders 24 and 2 hours out, review prompts after, and messages that bring quiet clients back — sent automatically. You show up, the app does the rest.

Getting started

Live in three steps.

No paperwork. No approval queue. Sign up, set your profile, connect to your salon, take bookings.

01

Sign up as a nail artist

Email, Google or Apple. Pick your role. Verify with a six-digit code and you’re in.

02

Build your profile

Upload portfolio photos, add your services with prices, set your weekly hours. Skip the parts you’ll fill in later.

03

Join your salon & go live

Search for your salon and ask to join. Once you’re on their team, bookings start landing.
