Built for how nail spas actually run in America — the salon owns the floor, the artists work the chairs, and every booking has one home.

Everything you get

The whole floor, in one place.

Bookings, roster, dashboard, stock — each one used to live in its own notebook, WhatsApp thread or spreadsheet. Now they don’t.

One inbox for the salon

Clients book the salon, not a person. Assign whoever’s free. If they can’t take it, the request comes back to the inbox — never dies on you.

Website bookings, same inbox

People book from joveelink.com without downloading the app. Their name and phone land in the same inbox as everything else — no separate flow to check.

See where business slips

The dashboard names it: how many requests died waiting, split into nobody was assigned and artist never answered. Per artist. Fix what’s actually broken.

Managers and co-owners

Add team members by email as managers or co-owners. They take bookings, assign artists and approve requests when you’re not there. Only the owner changes the team.

Your artists, one place

Your whole roster in one search. Filter by skills, experience or who’s free today. Review artists asking to join. Open any artist’s week to see their schedule.

Inventory that updates itself

Stock levels adjust as appointments are billed. Low-stock warnings. Quick stocktake to correct estimates. Recipes so a gel set deducts the right things.
