When they walk in

Check-in your customers do themselves.

Hand customers a phone or a tablet at the front desk. They check in with their phone number and join the line. Nobody stops mid-set to take a name.

  • New customers add their name, number, email and birthday. Returning customers just enter their number.
  • The screen shows who’s in line, by first name and last initial.
  • Customers pick English, Spanish or Vietnamese right on the check-in screen.
  • A 4-digit code locks the device to check-in, so customers can’t open the rest of the app.
  • Runs on iPad in portrait or landscape, with a large keypad on tablets.
  • Customers choose whether to get your offers by text or email. Nothing is ticked for them.

When a request comes in

Bookings with one home.

  • Bookings made on the web land in the same place. No app needed to book.
  • Clients can only book inside the business hours you set.
  • Owner, co-owners and managers see every change as it happens.
  • Alerts for new booking requests, join requests and messages, and you can reply to clients as the salon.

When the set is done

Checkout at the counter.

Ring up a walk-in, someone who checked in, or a booking. Add what was done and who did it, take the payment and print the ticket.

  • Every service goes on the ticket with its artist and its price.
  • Add a discount or a tip on card. Cash tips go straight to the artist.
  • Record how they paid: cash, card, gift card, or a split of cash and card.
  • Print every ticket on your counter printer.
  • Every sale of the day sits in one list.
  • Clients who book in the app can also pay in the app, by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna, Afterpay or Cash App Pay where available. Payouts reach your bank through Stripe.

Good to know

Questions salon owners ask.

Something else on your mind? Email support@joveear.ai or see the support page.

What does Jovée Link do for a nail salon?

It runs the salon day in one app: walk-in check-in, a walk-in queue, bookings, counter checkout, end-of-day close-out, weekly pay statements, customer campaigns, inventory and a dashboard. Your salon also gets a profile in Charlotte’s nail community, where clients find artists, try a look with AR Nail Preview and book.

How much does it cost?

Jovée Link is free to download and free to set up. There is no setup fee and no sales call. If you take payments in the app, Stripe’s processing fee applies, and Jovée Link currently charges no platform fee on top.

Do my customers need the app?

No. Walk-ins check in on your phone or tablet with their phone number, and clients can book on the web without downloading anything. Clients who do use the app can also follow your artists, chat with you and pay in the app.

What do I need for walk-in check-in?

A phone or a tablet at the front desk. You choose a 4-digit code, hand the device over, and customers check themselves in. The code keeps them out of the rest of the app. On iPad, check-in runs in portrait or landscape with a large keypad.

How do pay statements work?

Each week the app builds a pay statement for every artist from the sales you rang up: their sales, their commission and their card tips. You set the commission rate and how pay is split between check and cash, and each statement shows the payday.

How do in-app payments and payouts work?

You set up payouts through Stripe inside the app and give your business and bank details to Stripe directly. After an appointment you send an itemized bill, the client pays in the app, and the money reaches your bank on your Stripe payout schedule. You can also take payment at the counter and ring it up in Checkout.

Can I send texts and emails to my customers?

Yes. Customers choose at check-in whether to get your offers by text or email. You can send one-off campaigns, or switch on automatic ones for birthdays, review requests and customers who haven’t visited in 30 days. Email campaigns work from day one, and texting is switched on salon by salon.

Which languages does it support?

English, Spanish and Vietnamese. Customers pick their language on the check-in screen, and clients see your standard services in their own app language.

Is Jovée Link only in Charlotte?

Jovée Link is live in Charlotte, NC and is growing city by city.
